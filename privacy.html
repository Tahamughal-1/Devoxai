Privacy Policy

How DevoxAI collects, uses, and protects your information

October 9, 2026 Taha Imran

At DevoxAI, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by DevoxAI and how we use it.

DevoxAI is a platform dedicated to helping businesses grow using Artificial Intelligence, SaaS tools, and modern marketing strategies. We share practical guides, tool reviews, case studies, and insights covering AI Tools for Business, SaaS Platforms, B2B Sales Strategies, Marketing Automation, and Growth Case Studies.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. The types of information we may collect include:

  • Personal Identification Information: Name, email address, or other details provided voluntarily when you subscribe to our newsletter or contact us.
  • Log Files: Like many standard websites, DevoxAI follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites.
  • Cookies and Web Beacons: We use cookies to store information about visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited.

2. Google AdSense and DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our website and other sites on the internet.

However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

3. Our Advertising Partners

Some of advertising partners on our site may use cookies and web beacons. Our advertising partners include Google AdSense. Each of our advertising partners has their own Privacy Policy for their policies on user data.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our website
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our website and content
  • Understand and analyze how you use our website
  • Develop new products, services, features, and functionality
  • Send you emails regarding updates, guides, and marketing automation insights
  • Serve relevant advertisements through Google AdSense

5. Third Party Privacy Policies

DevoxAI's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information.

6. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity. DevoxAI does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

7. Online Privacy Policy Only

This Privacy Policy applies only to our online activities and is valid for visitors to our website with regards to the information that they shared and/or collect in DevoxAI. This policy is not applicable to any information collected offline or via channels other than this website.

8. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, we advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at: taha@devoxai.com
